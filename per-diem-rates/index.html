Per Diem Rates Lookup

Federal per diem rates for FY2027 start at $113 a night for lodging and $68 a day for meals and incidentals, the standard CONUS rate. 297 destinations are listed separately and run higher, up to $499 a night. Use the lookup above to see any CONUS city month by month for FY2026 or FY2027.

Updated 16 September 2026

How to read the rates

Every destination returns two numbers: a lodging maximum per night and an M&IE amount per day. Lodging is a ceiling on actual spend, before hotel taxes. M&IE is a flat daily allowance that needs no receipts.

The lodging figure is set by month, so the lookup shows twelve values. The rate that applies is the one for the month each night falls in. M&IE is one figure for the whole year and falls into one of five tiers: $68, $74, $80, $86 or $92. Source: the GSA Per Diem API.

Standard rate vs listed destinations

GSA lists 297 CONUS destinations for FY2027 and 298 for FY2026. Anywhere not on that list, which is most of the country, uses the standard rate instead.

Frequently asked questions

What is an appropriate per diem rate?

For federal travel the appropriate rate is whatever GSA publishes for your destination and month: there is no judgement call. For FY2027 that is $113 lodging and $68 M&IE at the standard CONUS rate, or a higher figure at one of the 297 listed destinations. Private employers often adopt the same table because it keeps reimbursements inside IRS limits without receipts.

What is the standard per diem rate for 2026?

The FY2026 standard CONUS per diem is $110 a night for lodging and $68 a day for meals and incidentals, in effect from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026. On the first and last day of travel the M&IE drops to $51. FY2027, which starts 1 October 2026, raises lodging to $113 and leaves M&IE at $68.

How do you calculate per diem?

Price each night’s lodging at the maximum for that destination and that calendar month, then add M&IE for each day: the full tier amount on full days and 75% of it on the departure and return days. A three-night stay at $113 lodging and $68 M&IE gives $339 + $51 + $68 + $68 + $51, a trip maximum of $577.

How much does the IRS allow for per diem?

The IRS does not publish its own rate table for domestic travel; it accepts the federal per diem rates GSA sets. An employer reimbursing at or below those rates can treat the payment as a non-taxable business expense without collecting meal receipts. Anything paid above the published rate is treated as taxable wages unless the excess is supported by actual receipts.

What is the federal per diem rate?

The federal per diem rate is the maximum daily travel reimbursement GSA sets for each CONUS location. It has two parts: a nightly lodging ceiling and a daily meals and incidentals allowance. For FY2027 the standard rate is $113 and $68. Listed destinations carry their own figures, with lodging running from just above standard up to $499 a night.

What is the IRS per diem rate for meals in 2026?

For FY2026 the meals and incidentals rate is $68 a day at the standard CONUS rate, with higher-cost destinations at $74, $80, $86 or $92. The IRS uses the same GSA figures for domestic travel. The first and last day of a trip are paid at 75%, so $51 on the standard rate.

What are the 2026 per diem rates?

FY2026 rates run 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026: standard lodging $110 a night, standard M&IE $68 a day, and 298 listed destinations with their own rates. M&IE tiers are $68, $74, $80, $86 and $92. Many listed destinations change lodging rate by month, so check the specific month in the lookup above.

What is the federal limit on per diem rates?

The published rate is the limit: for FY2027 that is $113 a night for lodging at the standard rate, or the destination figure where one is listed. Lodging is reimbursed at actual cost up to that ceiling, never above it, with hotel taxes paid separately. M&IE is a fixed amount rather than a cap and is paid in full regardless of what you spend.
