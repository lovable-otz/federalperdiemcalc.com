Why trust these numbers

Every rate on this site is pulled from the GSA Per Diem API, the same dataset behind the rate tables on gsa.gov. Nothing is typed in by hand. The current data was fetched on 14 September 2026 and covers FY2026 and FY2027 CONUS lodging, M&IE tiers and meal breakdowns: 298 listed destinations for FY2026 and 297 for FY2027, plus the standard rate that applies everywhere else.

GSA publishes the next fiscal year each August, and the dataset here is refreshed from the API at that point. The calculation rules (fiscal year starting 1 October, lodging priced by the month of the night, 75% M&IE on the first and last travel day) follow the Federal Travel Regulation. Federal Per Diem Calc is an independent site and is not affiliated with the U.S. General Services Administration.

Common questions

What are the new per diem rates for 2025?

FY2025 set the standard CONUS rate at $110 a night for lodging and $68 a day for meals and incidentals, effective 1 October 2024. The M&IE tiers ran $68, $74, $80, $86 and $92. More in Per Diem Rates 2025 (FY2025).

What is the IRS meal per diem rate for 2025?

$68 a day at the standard CONUS rate for FY2025, with higher-cost destinations at $74 through $92. More in Per Diem Rates 2025 (FY2025).

How do I calculate a per diem?

Price each night’s lodging at the destination maximum for that calendar month, then add M&IE for each day: the full amount on full days, 75% on the departure and return days. More in Per Diem Rates 2025 (FY2025).

What is per diem for truckers in 2025?

Long-haul drivers are covered by the IRS special transportation industry per diem, which is a separate meal allowance from the GSA CONUS rates shown here and applies to workers subject to Department of Transportation hours-of-service rules. More in Per Diem Rates 2025 (FY2025).

What is a federal per diem rate?

It is the maximum daily travel reimbursement GSA publishes for each CONUS location, made up of a nightly lodging ceiling and a daily meals and incidentals allowance. More in Per Diem Rates 2025 (FY2025).

What is the federal per diem rate for meals in 2026?

$68 a day at the standard CONUS rate for FY2026, which runs 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026. Higher-cost destinations fall into the $74, $80, $86 or $92 tiers. More in Per Diem Rates 2025 (FY2025).
